# CarePlus clinic and personal health records

## Clinic records

Open **Clinic records** to view read-only records created by CarePlus after completed appointments. A record can show the patient, doctor, visit type and time, diagnosis, doctor comments, charges, and prescribed medicines with dosage, quantity, and instructions. The details can be downloaded directly as a formatted PDF report.

A patient may review the doctor once for each clinic record. A review has a rating from one to five stars, an optional written description, and an option to submit anonymously. Submitted reviews appear on the doctor's public details page.

Common questions: Where are my consultation notes? How do I download my clinic report? How do I see my prescription? Chinese: 查看诊所记录、下载诊疗报告、查看处方、评价医生。

## Personal health records

Open **Personal health records** to manage information entered by the patient. Patients can record blood-pressure readings, maintain current allergy and condition tags, and upload PDF documents from other hospitals or clinics. Each uploaded PDF is stored as a separate record and can be viewed or deleted. Future dates and times are not accepted for health readings.

Clinic records and personal health records are different: clinic records are produced by CarePlus after visits and are read-only, while personal health records are added and managed by the patient.
