# CarePlus family profiles and notifications

## Family profiles

Open **Family profiles** to create or manage a family group. Members are invited using the email address of an existing CarePlus patient. The recipient is not added until the invitation is accepted. Family relationships are Parent or Child, and a member may use a family display name to make people with the same full name easier to distinguish.

The family owner can rename or delete the family, invite members, and remove a member with confirmation. A member can leave with confirmation. If the owner is the only member, leaving deletes the family without an ownership transfer. If other members exist, ownership must be transferred before the owner leaves.

Common questions: How do I invite a family member? Why is an invitation pending? How do I leave a family? Chinese: 家庭档案、邀请家人、接受邀请、退出家庭。

## Notifications and reminders

Open **Notifications** from the bell icon or navigation. CarePlus notifications can include family invitations and invitation results, appointment reminders, payment reminders, medicine-order updates, and clinic announcements. A notification can be opened, marked as read, selected with other notifications, or deleted. The page can sort newest-to-oldest or oldest-to-newest and filter by a start and end date.

Clinic announcements are also available on the public **Clinic announcements** page. Notification preferences control the unread badge behavior. If a patient chooses no notification alerts, notifications can still be stored, but they are treated as read so the red unread badge is not shown.
