# CarePlus prescribed medicine, cart, and orders

## Medicine and cart

Open **Medicine** to browse medicines prescribed through CarePlus and view medicine details. Add an eligible medicine to the cart, change its quantity, or remove it before checkout. If the cart is empty, use the link in the cart panel to return to Medicine.

For safety, the same patient can purchase at most five units of the same medicine in the same month through the portal. A patient who needs more should contact or visit the clinic counter. Chinese: 处方药、药物、购物车、每月数量限制。

## Checkout and placing an order

At checkout, choose clinic pickup or delivery when available. A delivery order requires a saved delivery address and may include a delivery fee. Select a supported payment method and review the order before placing it. A successfully placed order is stored in the database and opens **Order history**.

## Order history and tracking

Open **Order history** or **Track order** to view order items, total, fulfilment method, payment method, and current status. Pickup orders can show **Ready to pick up**. Delivery tracking shows the current medicine or courier location when location data is available, rather than only the destination.

For Cash on delivery, payment remains pending until a delivery order is delivered. For a pickup order using cash payment, payment remains pending until the order is picked up. Only then does the payment appear in Payment history.
