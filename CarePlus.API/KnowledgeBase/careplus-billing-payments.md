# CarePlus bills, payments, and receipts

## Outstanding bills

Open **Outstanding bill** or **Outstanding invoices** to see unpaid clinic invoices loaded from the database. Invoices are ordered by due date by default so the bill due soonest appears first. A user can switch between oldest-to-latest and latest-to-oldest ordering and can open Payment history from the page.

An unpaid invoice starts receiving a late fee after its due date. The CarePlus rule is RM10.00 for each overdue day. The amount due is the original invoice total plus the accumulated late fee. If an overdue bill exists, protected patient features may remain locked until all overdue bills are paid; account settings remain available so the patient can manage payment details.

## Paying and payment history

Open an invoice and choose an available payment method, such as a saved card or FPX, then review the charge before confirming. Open **Payment history** to see completed clinic payments and paid medicine purchases. Payment history can be sorted oldest-to-latest or latest-to-oldest. A receipt can be downloaded as a PDF where available.

Cash-on-delivery medicine orders do not appear as completed payments before delivery or pickup. They enter Payment history only after the order is marked delivered or picked up and the cash payment is completed.
