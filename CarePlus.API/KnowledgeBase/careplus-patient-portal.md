# CarePlus patient portal

CarePlus is a clinic patient portal. Patients can register, verify their email, sign in, maintain their profile, book appointments, view upcoming visits, read clinic records, manage personal health records, browse prescribed medicine, place medicine orders, review bills and payments, receive notifications, and manage family profiles.

## Appointments

Open **Booking** to choose the patient, visit type, doctor, date, and an available time slot. Open **Upcoming visits** to view, reschedule, or cancel a future appointment. Available times shown by the booking page come from the clinic scheduling system.

## Records

Open **Clinic records** for read-only notes created by CarePlus after completed appointments. A clinic record can contain visit details, diagnoses, and prescribed medicine. Open **Personal health records** to manage personal readings and uploaded PDF documents. Patients should not use the chatbot to diagnose symptoms or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

## Medicine and orders

Open **Medicine** to browse medicine prescribed through CarePlus. Items can be added to the cart and ordered for clinic pickup or delivery when those options are available. Open **Track order** or order history to check an existing medicine order.

## Billing and notifications

Open **Outstanding bill** to view unpaid invoices and continue to payment. Open **Payment history** to view completed payments and available receipts. Notifications contain clinic announcements, appointment reminders, payment reminders, and family invitations. Notification display follows the patient's app preferences.

## Support and emergencies

For account-specific problems, uncertain clinic policies, or information not shown in CarePlus, contact the clinic through **Contact us**. The chatbot provides general CarePlus guidance only. For urgent or potentially life-threatening symptoms, contact local emergency services immediately.
