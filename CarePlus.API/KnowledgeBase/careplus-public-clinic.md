# CarePlus doctors, clinic information, and support

## Doctors

The public **Doctors** page lists doctors from the CarePlus database. Open a doctor to see available profile details such as specialty, qualification, experience, working hours, and patient reviews. A doctor can also be selected during appointment booking.

Current availability depends on the selected booking date, session, and time slot. To find which doctor is available, sign in and open **Booking**, select the date and morning or night session, then view the database-backed available slots. Select **Any available doctor** to let CarePlus assign an available doctor. The chatbot must not claim that a particular doctor is available without checking the booking schedule.

## Public pages

Guests can use the landing page, Doctors, About us, Contact us, and Clinic announcements without signing in. Patient-only pages such as booking, records, family, medicine orders, bills, payments, and account settings require an authenticated patient account.

## Contact and help

Open **Contact us** for the clinic's current email, telephone number, office address, opening hours, map, and frequently asked questions. The office shown in the portal is at Jalan Genting Kelang, Setapak, 53300 Kuala Lumpur, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur. The page currently states that the clinic is open 24 hours every day and that night visits are handled by the after-hours GP team.

For patient-specific account problems, uncertain clinic policies, or information not shown in CarePlus, contact the clinic directly. The chatbot provides general CarePlus guidance only and cannot access or reveal private patient records. For urgent or potentially life-threatening symptoms, contact local emergency services immediately.
