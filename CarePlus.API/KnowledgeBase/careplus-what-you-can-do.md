# What you can do in CarePlus

Use this when someone asks what can you do, what can I do, what can this website do, what are you able to do, how can you help, or what features are available. Chinese wording: 你能做什么、我可以做什么、这个网站能做什么、你能帮我什么。

After signing in, a patient can:

- Book a visit, then view, reschedule, or cancel it. Open [Booking](/book) and [Upcoming visits](/visits).
- Compare doctors, specialties, and reviews. Open [Doctors](/doctors).
- Read clinic notes, diagnoses, charges, and prescribed medicine, then download a PDF. Open [Clinic records](/clinic-records).
- Add blood-pressure readings, allergy or condition tags, and outside PDF files. Open [Personal health records](/records).
- Browse prescribed medicine, add it to the cart, and order clinic pickup or delivery. Open [Medicine](/medicine), [Cart](/cart), and [Order history](/orders).
- Pay an unpaid clinic bill with a saved card or FPX, then download a receipt from payment history. Open [Outstanding bill](/invoices) and [Payment history](/payments).
- Create a family group, invite a patient by email, and accept or manage invitations. Open [Family profiles](/family).
- Read appointment reminders, payment reminders, order updates, and announcements. Open [Notifications](/notifications) and [Clinic announcements](/announcements).
- Update profile details, addresses, saved cards, language, appearance, password, and two-factor authentication. Open [Account](/account).

Before signing in, a visitor can open the landing page, Doctors, About us, Contact us, Clinic announcements, registration, and login. Booking, records, medicine checkout, orders, bills, family, and account pages need a login. Open [Register](/register), [Login](/login), and [Contact us](/contact).

The assistant can explain these steps. It cannot diagnose symptoms, open another person's records, or change an account by itself. For urgent symptoms, contact local emergency services.
