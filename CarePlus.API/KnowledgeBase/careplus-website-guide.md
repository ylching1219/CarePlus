# CarePlus website guide and common questions

## What can the CarePlus chatbot help with?

The CarePlus assistant can explain how to use the patient website: registration, email verification, login, password reset, account settings, two-factor authentication, appointment booking, upcoming visits, doctors, clinic records, personal health records, prescribed medicine, cart and checkout, order tracking, family profiles, bills, payments, notifications, clinic announcements, contact details, location, and opening hours. It provides navigation and general clinic information but does not diagnose illness, reveal private patient data, or replace a healthcare professional.

## Which doctor is available?

Doctor and time-slot availability changes with the selected date and session, so it should not be guessed from static chatbot knowledge. Sign in, open **Booking**, choose the date and morning or night session, and CarePlus will load the currently available time slots from the clinic database. Choose **Any available doctor** if the earliest suitable time matters more than a specific doctor. To compare doctor specialties, qualifications, experience, working hours, and reviews, open **Doctors** and select a doctor. Common wording: which doctor is free, available doctor today, doctor schedule, doctor working hours, 哪位医生有空、医生时间表。

## How do I make a booking?

Sign in and open **Booking** or **Book a visit**. Select who the visit is for, a date and session, a doctor or any available doctor, the visit type, and an open time slot. Review the summary and confirm. The appointment then appears in **Upcoming visits**. If no slot appears, try another date, session, or doctor. Common wording: make booking, book appointment, schedule visit, reserve a doctor, clinic appointment, 预约、挂号。

## How do I contact the clinic?

The Contact us page also provides a clickable map, office address, clinic hours, and frequently asked questions. Common wording: contact clinic, clinic phone, telephone number, email address, call CarePlus, 联系诊所、诊所电话、诊所电邮。

## What are the clinic hours?

The Contact us page states **Open 24 hours, every day**. Night visits are handled by the after-hours GP team. Actual doctor availability still depends on the schedule and open appointment slots shown on the Booking page.

## How do I register and verify my account?

Open **Register**, enter the required account information, and submit the form. CarePlus emails a verification button and six-digit code to the registered address. Complete verification before signing in. The username must be unique and can be used for login. The Malaysian identity card number and email must also be unique. If the email does not arrive, check the address and spam folder, then use the resend option where shown.

## How do I log in or reset my password?

The login field accepts either the username or email address. If the password is forgotten, open **Forgot password**, enter the account email, request the code, verify it, and enter the new password twice. A valid password needs at least eight characters with an uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and symbol.

## Where can I change my account settings?

Select the profile picture and choose **View profile**. The Account page contains Profile, Preferences, Payment, and Security areas. A patient can update permitted personal details, phone number, addresses, default delivery address, profile photo, saved payment cards, appearance, language, notification preferences, password, and two-factor authentication. Gender and date of birth are derived from the Malaysian identity card number rather than changed manually.

## How do I view, reschedule, or cancel an appointment?

Open **Upcoming visits** to see confirmed future appointments. Select a visit to view its details. Use **Reschedule** to choose another available slot or **Cancel** to cancel after confirmation. CarePlus limits a patient to three upcoming appointments, one appointment in each same-day session, three reschedules for the same visit per day, and may block new bookings after repeated same-day cancellations.

## Where are my clinic records and prescriptions?

Open **Clinic records** after a completed appointment. Choose **View details** for diagnosis, doctor comments, charges, and prescribed medicines. Choose **Download PDF** for a report containing the record and medication details. A doctor can be reviewed once per clinic record with one to five stars, an optional description, and an optional anonymous setting.

## What are personal health records?

Open **Personal health records** for information managed by the patient rather than records created by CarePlus. Patients can add blood-pressure readings, current allergy or condition tags, and PDF documents from outside hospitals or clinics. A reading cannot use a future date or time. Each PDF is stored as a separate record with View and Delete actions.

## How do medicine purchases work?

Open **Medicine** to browse eligible prescribed medicines, view details, and add items to the cart. At checkout, choose clinic pickup or delivery, select an address when delivery is used, choose a payment method, and confirm the order. The portal allows at most five units of the same medicine for the same patient in one month; contact the clinic counter if more is needed. Open **Order history** or **Track order** after placing the order.

## How do pickup, delivery, and cash payment work?

A pickup order can show **Ready to pick up** when prepared. A delivery order moves through its delivery statuses and can show current tracking information where available. For Cash on delivery, the payment remains pending until delivery. For a pickup order paid in cash, it remains pending until pickup. The completed payment appears in Payment history only after delivery or pickup.

## How do I pay a bill or download a receipt?

Open **Outstanding bill** to see unpaid invoices and late charges. Select an invoice, review the charge, choose an available card or FPX method, and confirm payment. The overdue fee is RM10.00 per day after the due date. Open **Payment history** for paid consultations and completed medicine payments, then use the receipt action to download a PDF where available.

## How do family profiles work?

Open **Family profiles** to create a group or manage an existing one. Invite an existing CarePlus patient by email and choose Parent or Child. The recipient joins only after accepting the invitation. Invitations and invitation results appear in Notifications. Members can use a family display name. Leaving, removing a member, transferring ownership, or deleting a family requires the relevant confirmation and ownership rules.

## What appears in Notifications?

Notifications can include appointment reminders, payment reminders, medicine-order updates, family invitations and results, and clinic announcements. Open the bell or **Notifications** page to read, mark as read, select, sort, filter by date, or delete notices. Clinic announcements can also be read from the public **Clinic announcements** page. Turning off notification alerts hides the unread badge by treating incoming notices as read; it does not prevent notices from being stored.

## What can guests access?

Guests can access the landing page, Doctors, About us, Contact us, Clinic announcements, registration, login, password reset, and the general chatbot. Patient features such as booking, upcoming visits, account settings, clinic records, personal health records, family, medicine checkout, orders, bills, payments, and personal notifications require login.

## How do language, appearance, search, and navigation work?

CarePlus supports English and Chinese and light or dark appearance. These can be changed from the header or account preferences, depending on screen size and login state. The header search opens a search field for website pages and keeps recent searches. The Services menu groups links for appointments, records, bills, family, announcements, notifications, and medicine-related pages. Breadcrumbs show the path used to reach the current page.
