# CarePlus appointments and booking

## How to make a booking

To book an appointment, sign in and open **Booking** or **Book a visit**. The booking flow asks who the visit is for, the date and morning or night session, a doctor or any available doctor, the visit type, and an available time slot. Review the summary and confirm the booking. The doctor list and available time slots are loaded from the CarePlus clinic scheduling database.

## Appointment types and limits

CarePlus supports the active visit types shown on the booking page, including General consultation, Body check, and Mental wellness when they are available. A patient can have at most three upcoming appointments. On the same day, a patient can book at most one appointment in each clinic session. The system prevents two patients from taking the same doctor time slot.

## Upcoming visits

Open **Upcoming visits** to see future appointments and their doctor, visit type, date, time, and status. A future visit can be rescheduled or cancelled from this page. The same appointment can be rescheduled up to three times in one day. Repeated cancellations may temporarily block new bookings until the next day. After a successful booking, **View booking** opens Upcoming visits.

## Appointment check-in and clinic queue

CarePlus uses a 30-minute check-in window for scheduled appointments. Check-in opens 10 minutes before the appointment and closes 20 minutes after the appointment start time. The patient receives an in-app check-in reminder when the window opens. Open **Clinic queue** from that notification, from **Services > Appointments > Clinic queue**, or from the **Explore CarePlus** queue shortcut. Only check in after arriving at the clinic.

After successful check-in, CarePlus creates a live queue entry and shows the queue number, number of patients ahead, estimated waiting time, current queue status, and assigned consultation room when available. Queue information refreshes automatically. Common statuses include Waiting, Called, and In consultation.

If the patient does not check in before the 30-minute window closes, CarePlus automatically cancels the appointment and sends an in-app notification explaining that it was cancelled because check-in was missed. The patient can then make another booking. If check-in is unavailable unexpectedly while the patient is at the clinic, contact the clinic counter for help.
